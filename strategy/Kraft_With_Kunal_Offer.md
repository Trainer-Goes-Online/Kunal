## How it works

Four phases. The man can feel each one end and the next begin. The 90-Day Reset runs the first two. The 6-Month Transformation runs all four.

**Phase 1, Assessment & Reset (Weeks 1 to 4).** Full workup: training history, body composition, schedule, eating habits, injuries, medical history, current blood work. The plan gets built to his real week. Training starts in three days. Food gets structured without pulling anything he will not sustain.

**Phase 2, Fat Loss (Weeks 5 to 12).** A real but liveable deficit. Two numbers run the show: protein and steps. Training lasts for three to four days and gets harder on purpose. Weekly check-in, weekly adjustment.

**Phase 3, Recomposition (Weeks 13 to 20).** Food comes back up. Strength work ramps. The body gets built, not just lighter. Travel weeks and restaurant weeks get planned for, not feared.

**Phase 4, Lifestyle Lock (Weeks 21 to 26).** He learns to run his own week. Markers retested against the baseline. The result is made permanent, and the retainer conversation opens here.
