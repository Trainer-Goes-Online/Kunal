THE APPROACH (read once)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We use ONE consistent, relatable persona, photographed in a believable moment, with
a tasteful on-brand INFOGRAPHIC element built INTO the scene — so the image alone
tells that section's story even before the copy is read. This mirrors the reference
funnel (suvidhi.innohealth.co.in): the same woman in each section + one clean data
element (a "BLOOD TEST REPORT" card with a NORMAL→OPTIMAL slider; a SPIKE→CRASH
glucose curve; a broken absorption chain by a supplement bottle).

We do the same, but for our man and our brand. Keep the man IDENTICAL in all three
images — same face, same warm expression, same build, same wardrobe, same grade.

HOW TO USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Generate PROMPT 1 first — it LOCKS the man.
2. For PROMPT 2 and 3, in the same chat, attach the Prompt-1 image and add:
   "Use the SAME man as the attached image — identical face, warmth, build, wardrobe
   and colour grade. Only change the scene and the infographic as described." Reuse
   the same seed / character-reference if your tool supports it.
3. Save as: public/infographics/problem.png · stakes.png · plan.png
4. Short, correctly-spelled labels ON the infographic are welcome (the reference uses
   them). If letters garble, reply: "Redraw the labels cleanly, exactly spelled: <words>."
5. ASPECT: landscape 3:2 (≈1536×1024) or 4:3. Cinematic, with breathing room on the
   side where the infographic sits (it appears to the RIGHT of the copy on desktop).

WHO THE MAN IS (the fix from v5: warm & relatable, NOT a stern "uncle")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
He should feel like a genuinely likeable, warm, successful man you'd want to talk to —
approachable and human, not stern, not intimidating, not a glossy model. Kind, gentle
eyes; an easy, warm presence; the quiet confidence of someone who's done well but is
real and down-to-earth. Tired in the tough sections, hopeful in the payoff — always
warm. The FULL description is written into every prompt below so you don't have to
assemble anything.

PROMPT 1 — §2 "THE PROBLEM"   (his packed day always wins; no room for his health)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STORY THIS IMAGE MUST CARRY (no reading needed): "He runs everything with discipline —
except this. His day is so completely full of work that there's no slot left for his
own health. The calendar always wins."
================================================================================
A photorealistic, cinematic, premium advertising photograph — landscape 3:2.

THE MAN: A warm, likeable, successful Indian businessman, around 40 (reads 35–50). He
is NOT a fitness model and NOT stern — he's a real, approachable, down-to-earth man
with a genuinely kind, gentle face and soft, warm, tired eyes. Realistic mid-life
build: medium/softer frame with a slight belly and a fuller, rounder friendly face —
a man who eats well and sits in meetings, roughly 26–30% body fat, comfortable, not
lean, not toned. Warm medium-brown skin, healthy and well-kept. Neat modern short
black hair with just a light touch of grey at the temples (young and current, not
heavily greyed). Clean or very light, tidy stubble — soft, not a heavy beard. Relaxed,
warm micro-expression even when weary. Quiet-luxury smart-casual: a well-fitted, soft
warm-charcoal or sand henley/linen shirt with sleeves pushed up, a simple premium
watch, a subtle wedding band. He looks like someone you'd instantly like and trust.

THE SCENE: Late evening. He sits at an elegant executive desk, mid-work, composed and
capable but softly weary — maybe resting his temple on two fingers, looking gently
tired rather than frowning. Signs of a day with no time for himself: a half-finished
cup of chai and two plain biscuits pushed aside (a skipped, rushed meal), a phone lit
with notifications, papers, an open laptop. A gym bag sits UNTOUCHED and forgotten in
the softly-lit corner.

THE INTEGRATED INFOGRAPHIC (the story element): Floating beside him as a tasteful
glowing brass-gold holographic overlay, a 24-HOUR DAY RING that is COMPLETELY, densely
packed — segment after segment filled with small work icons (meetings, calls, a
briefcase, a flight, a laptop, a car), every hour booked, all glowing brass. ONE thin,
dim, almost-invisible sliver is left, and squeezed into it is a small faded dumbbell
icon in dull grey, crushed to the very edge with no room. A small clean label reads
"24 HOURS"; a tiny muted tag by the sliver reads "HEALTH." Instant read: his whole day
is full of everything except the one thing his body needs.

THE LOOK / GRADE: Premium, editorial, cinematic — a luxury-brand commercial, not a
stock photo. An expensive, warm, masculine modern interior (dark home study / penthouse
office) in deep graphite / near-black tones (#17181C–#1E2026) with warm brass-gold
lamplight (#C9A24B / #E7CE93) as the single accent raking across the scene. Shallow
depth of field, soft cinematic bokeh, fine film grain, restrained and expensive. The
infographic glows in that same brass-gold and feels integrated into the real photo.
ONE accent colour only — brass. No rainbow, no neon, no clip-art.

A photorealistic, cinematic, premium advertising photograph — landscape 3:2.
USE THE SAME MAN as Prompts 1 & 2 (attach an earlier image): identical warm, likeable
